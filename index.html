Welcome to TaskTree!

"You will never feel truly satisfied by work until you are satisfied by life."
– Heather Schuck

A digital plant that lives in your web-browser to help students manage school-life balance which will help maintain the motivation to continue putting effort into academics as well as keep their other time management tools effective.

It grows based on how you spend your time. You can input tasks and label them as either 'academic' or 'personal', and the digital tree will change whether your activities are balanced (a nice green tree) or if there is an imbalance in academics (a grey dead tree), or a creative/personal imbalance (wild colours). The different stages of the tree communicate to students how much of their time is being spent on too much of the same thing which can often be a cause of stress or procrastination.

Look down below and you will find TaskTree's timer functions! TaskTree automatically balances your study time to prevent burnout. After 30 minutes of focused work, a pop-up tells you to rest and automatically starts a 15-minute break. Once your break finishes, another alert asks for your next task and resets the clock back to 30 minutes, keeping your routine going with zero hassle!
